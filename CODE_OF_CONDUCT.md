# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the
project. Many users are parents of children with type 1 diabetes: be kind about their
situation and never give medical advice.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, through the contact details on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may
remove comments, issues or contributions, or block people, that do not follow this code.
